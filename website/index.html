Notes that stay where you actually look.

Stickit puts paper sticky notes right on your Android home screen. Tilted, taped, soft colours. Write a list, tick it off from the widget, or keep a verse in front of you all day.

Download for Android Free · Android APK · 16 MB · No account, no ads

What it does

A notes app that lives on your home screen.

01

Widgets that look like paper

Every note can be pinned as a resizable widget. Checklists can be ticked straight from the home screen without opening the app.

02

On your lock screen

Mark a note for the lock screen and it's the first thing you see when you pick up your phone.

03

A verse every hour

Optional Bible and Quran notes that refresh on their own. Don't want one? Delete it, and bring it back any time from settings.

On a real home screen

Pick a colour. Stick it anywhere.

Installing

Takes about a minute.

  1. Download the APK from this page on your phone.
  2. Open it. If Android asks, allow installs from this source.
  3. Open Stickit and write your first note.
  4. Long-press it and choose Add to home screen.
